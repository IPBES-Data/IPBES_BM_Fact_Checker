# ⚠️ THE FILES IN THIS DIRECTORY ARE SYNTHETIC

`R1_GA1.csv`, `R2_GA1.csv` and `gold_GA1.csv` were **generated by Claude for a
plumbing test**, on request. They are **not human judgements** and must not be
treated as a gold standard.

Every row carries `reviewer = SYNTHETIC-R1` / `SYNTHETIC-R2` and a `note` saying
so, so no row can be mistaken for real review work.

## What they actually are

One model read each row's claim, title and abstract from the blinded template
and assigned a verdict — genuinely, not at random, and blind to every model
label (the template carries none). `R1` is that read. `R2` is the **same read
with the borderline cases resolved the other way**: rows where the judgement
was genuinely close take the alternative reading. That yields a realistic
disagreement rate (72.5% raw agreement, 55 of 200 rows) and exercises the
adjudication and human-ceiling code paths.

**This is one judge, not two.** κ computed from these files measures how often
one reader's confident calls differ from its own borderline calls. It does not
measure inter-annotator agreement, and the number must never be quoted as a
human ceiling.

## Why this does not substitute for the real thing

The whole point of the gold standard is to break the circle in which every
number measures agreement with an LLM. A gold standard produced by an LLM
leaves that circle intact — it just adds a step. Any benchmark run against
these files measures agreement with a second model, not correctness.
